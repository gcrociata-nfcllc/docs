title: 'Integration Roadmap'
---

The WPNScripts API integration process is completed in several stages, outlined below in sequence:

### Submit New Account Onboarding Form

<Card href="/">
  All new clients are required to complete and submit at least one **New Account onboarding form**\* (NAOF):

  → [WPN New Account Onboarding Form](https://wpnscripts.wellsrx.com/NewAccount)\
   → [WPPL New Account Onboarding Form](https://wplscripts.wellsrx.com/NewAccount)

  Filling out this form ensures that our New Accounts Onboarding team has all of the information needed in order to create your standard account. This is information such as:

  - Practice/organization name and address
  - Your preferred contact methods
  - Your points of contact for customer service and order-related inquiries
  - Prescriber information
  - Intended pharmacy designation<br/>
    <Icon icon="circle-exclamation" size={20}/> 
     _You may also hear the NAOF be referred to as the "New Account Set Up Form" (NASF)_ 
  <Note>
     If you have already submitted this form, skip and go to the next section
  </Note>
</Card>

### Complete WPNScripts API Setup Form

<Card href="/">
  This is a smaller form that is used by our Development Support team to gather basic details about your organization's API, such as:

  - Webhook URLs/keys
  - Points of contact for any communications related to the API
  - List of people that will need API-specific access to the WPNScripts web-based portal

  → [WPNScripts API Setup Form](https://forms.office.com/r/4fSZ8czQ5E)

  Filling out this form ensures that our Development Support team has all of the information needed in order to begin the integration process.
</Card>

### Schedule Integration Kickoff Call

<Card href="/">
  This is just an initial alignment meeting between our Development Support team and your organization to establish a shared understanding of goals, requirements, and next steps.

  Once both forms have been submitted, our Development Support team will reach out to coordinate this call.
</Card>

### Staging Environment Testing

<Card href="/">
  During this phase, we will ask you to submit several tests for each of the endpoints your organization plans to utilize to ensure that all aspects of your integration - including webhook events - are working properly.
  <Note>
     _If you plan to integrate with both pharmacy locations, we kindly ask that testing be performed in both staging environments._
  </Note>
</Card>

### Go Live!

<Card icon="hands-clapping" href="/">
  Once both parties feel confident that the integration is stable, you will be given access to our production environment(s).
</Card>
